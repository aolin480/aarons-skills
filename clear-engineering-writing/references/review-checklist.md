# Review Checklist

Before returning the artifact, confirm:

- The opening states the outcome, purpose, or main point.
- Every material claim is supported by supplied evidence or clearly labeled as a recommendation.
- Uncertainty, limitations, conditions, and exceptions remain intact.
- Technical identifiers and official names are unchanged.
- One concept does not have several interchangeable names.
- Sentences have one clear primary job.
- Actors and actions are explicit where they matter.
- Paragraphs start with their topic and contain one coherent subject.
- Procedures are ordered, actionable, and verifiable.
- Lists improve scanning instead of fragmenting ordinary prose.
- Filler, repetition, promotional adjectives, and empty introductions are removed.
- The tone and structure fit the artifact and audience.
- The result does not claim ASD-STE100 compliance.
